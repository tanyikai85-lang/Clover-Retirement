Key watch-outs (carried over from the original planner)
  • Spouse's CPF balances are estimated as identical to Yikai's — replace with actual figures for accuracy.
  • Pruwealth 3's leverage amount was not disclosed, so it's modelled at gross (not net) value.
  • The 15-year CPF gap: OA/SA are largely locked from retirement until CPF LIFE starts at 65 for both of you — don't count on CPF for early-retirement cash flow.
  • Endowus SRS incurs full tax plus a 5% penalty if withdrawn before the statutory retirement age — treated as a later-phase asset throughout.
  • The S$5m inheritance is contingent — timing, value and occurrence are all uncertain. Scenario B (or E) is the plan to actually rely on; C/D show the upside.
  • Scenario E's numbers are sensitive to two inputs you should set carefully: your real assumed sale price for The Line, and the price of the mortgage-free home you'd buy — both are editable above.
  • Mortgage rate is assumed fixed at 2% for the full term — real repricing risk exists; check against your actual bank package.
  • All growth rates, the SWR, and property/mortgage assumptions are editable — stress-test with lower rates before relying on this plan.
  • This page recalculates everything from the underlying formulas (monthly-compounding growth, amortization schedules) rather than copying static numbers, so figures land very close to — but not always cent-exact with — the original spreadsheet. Differences are typically under 0.2%.
